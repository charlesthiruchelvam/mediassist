# General Healthcare FAQs

## What are the hospital visiting hours?
General visiting hours are 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM daily.
ICU visiting is restricted to immediate family only, 15 minutes per visit.

## How do I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment by:
1. Calling our hotline: 011-100-2000 (8am to 5pm, Monday to Saturday)
2. Using the online portal at patient.hospital.lk
3. Walking in to the outpatient registration desk

## What documents should I bring to my appointment?
Please bring:
- National ID card or passport
- Insurance card if applicable
- Referral letter for specialist consultations
- Previous test results and medical records

## How do I get my test results?
Test results are available within 24-48 hours. You can collect them from
the laboratory counter or request them via the patient portal.

## What should I do in a medical emergency?
Call 1990 immediately or go to the nearest Accident and Emergency department.
Do not drive yourself if you are experiencing chest pain or difficulty breathing.
