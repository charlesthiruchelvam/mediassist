# Atorvastatin (Lipitor, Atorva)

## Drug Class
Statin — cholesterol-lowering medication

## Common Uses
- High cholesterol (hyperlipidaemia)
- Prevention of heart attack and stroke
- Cardiovascular disease risk reduction

## Standard Adult Dosage
Initial: 10mg to 20mg once daily.
High-risk patients: 40mg to 80mg once daily.
Take at any time of day, with or without food.

## How It Works
Atorvastatin blocks an enzyme in the liver that produces cholesterol,
reducing LDL (bad cholesterol) and triglycerides.

## Key Drug Interactions
- Amlodipine: increases atorvastatin levels — maximum 40mg dose.
- Clarithromycin/erythromycin: increases risk of muscle side effects.
- Grapefruit juice: increases atorvastatin levels — avoid large amounts.
- Warfarin: may enhance blood thinning effect — monitor INR closely.

## Who Should Avoid Atorvastatin
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women
- Patients with active liver disease
- Patients with unexplained elevated liver enzymes

## Warning Signs — Seek Immediate Care
- Unexplained muscle pain, tenderness, or weakness
- Dark brown or cola-coloured urine (sign of muscle breakdown)
- Yellowing of skin or eyes (jaundice)

## Monitoring Required
Liver function tests before starting and if symptoms develop.
Cholesterol levels checked every 3 to 6 months initially.
