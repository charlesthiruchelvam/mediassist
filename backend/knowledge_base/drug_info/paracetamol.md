# Paracetamol (Panadol, Acetaminophen)

## Drug Class
Analgesic and antipyretic (pain reliever and fever reducer)

## Common Uses
- Mild to moderate pain (headaches, toothache, muscle pain)
- Fever reduction in adults and children
- Safe first-line option for most patients

## Standard Adult Dosage
500mg to 1000mg every 4 to 6 hours as needed.
Maximum dose: 4000mg per day (4g). Do not exceed this limit.
Take with or without food.

## Key Drug Interactions
- Warfarin: prolonged use may enhance blood thinning effect.
- Alcohol: combined use significantly increases liver damage risk.
- Other paracetamol-containing products: do not double dose.

## Who Should Use With Caution
- Patients with liver disease or hepatitis
- Heavy alcohol users (more than 3 drinks per day)
- Patients with severe kidney disease

## Overdose Warning
Paracetamol overdose is a medical emergency even if the person feels fine.
Liver damage may not appear until 2 to 3 days later.
If overdose is suspected, go to emergency immediately.

## When to Seek Medical Advice
- Fever lasting more than 3 days
- Pain not relieved after 3 days of use
- Any signs of skin rash or allergic reaction
