# Ibuprofen (Brufen, Advil)

## Drug Class
NSAID — Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug

## Common Uses
- Mild to moderate pain relief (headaches, dental pain, muscle aches)
- Fever reduction
- Inflammation from arthritis or injury

## Standard Adult Dosage
200mg to 400mg every 4 to 6 hours as needed.
Maximum dose: 1200mg per day for over-the-counter use.
Always take with food or milk to protect the stomach.

## Important Drug Interactions
- Warfarin (blood thinner): significantly increases bleeding risk. Avoid.
- Aspirin: reduces aspirin's heart-protective effect.
- ACE inhibitors (e.g. lisinopril): may reduce effectiveness and affect kidneys.
- SSRIs (e.g. fluoxetine): combined use increases stomach bleeding risk.

## Who Should Avoid Ibuprofen
- People with active stomach ulcers
- Patients with severe kidney or liver disease
- Women in the last 3 months of pregnancy
- People with known allergy to NSAIDs or aspirin

## Warning Signs — Seek Immediate Care If
- Black or tarry stools (sign of internal bleeding)
- Vomiting blood
- Severe stomach pain
- Chest pain or shortness of breath after taking the medication
