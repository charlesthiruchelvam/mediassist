# Amoxicillin (Amoxil, Trimox)

## Drug Class
Penicillin antibiotic

## Common Uses
- Bacterial infections of the ear, nose, throat, and chest
- Urinary tract infections
- Skin infections
- Dental infections

## Important Note
Amoxicillin only works for bacterial infections.
It does NOT work for viral infections like colds or flu.
Always complete the full course even if you feel better.

## Standard Adult Dosage
250mg to 500mg every 8 hours, or 500mg to 875mg every 12 hours.
Always follow your doctor's prescribed dose and duration.

## Key Drug Interactions
- Warfarin: may increase bleeding risk.
- Methotrexate: increases toxicity risk.
- Oral contraceptives: may reduce effectiveness — use backup contraception.

## Who Should Avoid Amoxicillin
- Patients with known penicillin or amoxicillin allergy
- Patients with mononucleosis (glandular fever) — causes severe rash

## Side Effects to Watch For
- Mild diarrhea and stomach upset (common, usually mild)
- Skin rash — stop and seek medical advice
- Severe allergic reaction (difficulty breathing, swelling of face) —
  this is a medical emergency, call 1990 immediately

## Antibiotic Resistance Warning
Never take leftover antibiotics or share with others.
Never demand antibiotics for viral infections.
