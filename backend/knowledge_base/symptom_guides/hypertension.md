# Hypertension (High Blood Pressure) — Patient Guide

## What is Hypertension?
Blood pressure is measured in two numbers:
- Systolic (top number): pressure when heart beats
- Diastolic (bottom number): pressure when heart rests

## Blood Pressure Categories
- Normal: below 120/80 mmHg
- Elevated: 120-129 / below 80 mmHg
- Stage 1 hypertension: 130-139 / 80-89 mmHg
- Stage 2 hypertension: 140 or higher / 90 or higher mmHg
- Hypertensive crisis: above 180 / above 120 mmHg — emergency

## Symptoms
Hypertension is often called the silent killer because most people
have no symptoms. Severe hypertension may cause:
- Headaches (especially at the back of the head in the morning)
- Dizziness
- Blurred vision
- Chest pain
- Shortness of breath

## When to Go to Emergency
Blood pressure above 180/120 mmHg with any symptoms such as
chest pain, headache, vision changes, or difficulty breathing
is a hypertensive crisis. Call 1990 immediately.

## Lifestyle Management
1. Reduce salt intake to less than 5g per day
2. Exercise at least 30 minutes most days (brisk walking is excellent)
3. Maintain a healthy weight
4. Limit alcohol consumption
5. Stop smoking
6. Manage stress through relaxation techniques
7. Eat more fruits, vegetables, and low-fat dairy

## Medication Compliance
Never stop blood pressure medication without doctor's advice.
Blood pressure may feel normal but medication still required.
Take medication at the same time each day for best results.
