# Fever — Symptom Guide

## What is a Fever?
A fever is a body temperature above 38°C (100.4°F). It is usually a sign
that your body is fighting an infection.

## Fever Categories
- Low grade fever: 38°C to 38.5°C — monitor at home
- Moderate fever: 38.5°C to 39.5°C — treat with medication, stay hydrated
- High fever: above 39.5°C — seek medical attention
- Dangerous fever: above 40°C — go to emergency immediately

## Home Management for Mild Fever
1. Rest and drink plenty of fluids (water, coconut water, clear soups)
2. Take paracetamol (500mg to 1000mg every 4 to 6 hours for adults)
3. Use a cool damp cloth on the forehead
4. Wear light clothing and keep the room ventilated

## When to See a Doctor
- Fever lasting more than 3 days
- Fever above 39.5°C that does not come down with medication
- Fever with stiff neck, severe headache, or skin rash
- Fever with difficulty breathing
- Any fever in infants under 3 months old

## When to Go to Emergency
- Fever above 40°C
- Fever with confusion or loss of consciousness
- Fever with severe difficulty breathing
- Fever with seizures
