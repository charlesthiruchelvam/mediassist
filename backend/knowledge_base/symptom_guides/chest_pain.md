# Chest Pain — Symptom Guide

## IMPORTANT — When to Call Emergency Services Immediately
Call 1990 or go to emergency if chest pain is:
- Sudden, severe, or crushing
- Spreading to the arm, jaw, neck, or back
- Accompanied by shortness of breath, sweating, or nausea
- Occurring in someone with known heart disease
- Not relieved by rest within 15 minutes

These symptoms may indicate a heart attack. Do not drive yourself.
Call 1990 immediately.

## Common Non-Emergency Causes of Chest Pain
- Muscle strain from exercise or coughing
- Acid reflux or heartburn (burning sensation after eating)
- Costochondritis (inflammation of rib cartilage)
- Anxiety or panic attacks
- Respiratory infections

## Heartburn vs Heart Attack
Heartburn: burning sensation, worse after eating, relieved by antacids,
no spreading pain, no sweating.
Heart attack: pressure or squeezing sensation, may spread to arm or jaw,
not relieved by antacids, may cause sweating and nausea.

## When to See a Doctor (Non-Emergency)
- Chest pain that keeps recurring
- Chest pain after eating (possible reflux)
- Chest pain with mild breathing discomfort
- Chest pain in a young person after intense exercise

## Never Ignore Chest Pain
When in doubt, always seek medical evaluation.
It is always better to be checked and reassured than to wait.
