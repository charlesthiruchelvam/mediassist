# COVID-19 — Symptom Guide and Clinic Policy

## Common Symptoms
- Fever or chills
- Cough (usually dry)
- Fatigue
- Shortness of breath
- Loss of taste or smell
- Sore throat
- Headache
- Body aches
- Runny nose
- Nausea or diarrhea

## When to Seek Emergency Care Immediately
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath at rest
- Persistent chest pain or pressure
- Confusion or inability to stay awake
- Bluish lips or face
- Oxygen saturation below 94%

## Home Isolation Guidelines
- Isolate for at least 5 days from symptom onset
- Wear a mask around others in your home
- Rest and drink plenty of fluids
- Take paracetamol for fever and pain
- Monitor your oxygen levels with a pulse oximeter if available
- Seek medical care if symptoms worsen

## Clinic Attendance Policy
If you have COVID-19 symptoms, please call ahead before visiting
the clinic. We have separate assessment areas for respiratory illness.
Do not enter the waiting room without informing reception first.

## High Risk Groups
The following groups should seek early medical review:
- Adults over 60 years old
- Patients with diabetes, hypertension, or heart disease
- Patients with chronic lung conditions
- Immunocompromised patients
- Pregnant women

## Vaccination
COVID-19 vaccination is recommended for all eligible patients.
Speak to your doctor about booster doses and current guidelines.
