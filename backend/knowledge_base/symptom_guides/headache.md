# Headache — Symptom Guide

## Types of Headaches

### Tension Headache (most common)
- Feels like a tight band around the head
- Mild to moderate pressure, both sides of head
- Usually related to stress, poor posture, or screen time
- Treatment: paracetamol, rest, hydration, stretching

### Migraine
- Moderate to severe throbbing pain, usually one side
- May include nausea, vomiting, sensitivity to light and sound
- Can last 4 to 72 hours
- Triggers: stress, hormonal changes, certain foods, lack of sleep

### Cluster Headache
- Severe burning or stabbing pain around one eye
- Occurs in clusters — several times a day for weeks
- Requires specialist evaluation

## When to Seek Emergency Care Immediately
- Sudden severe headache described as the worst of your life
- Headache with stiff neck, fever, and sensitivity to light
  (possible meningitis)
- Headache after head injury
- Headache with confusion, vision changes, or weakness
- Headache with slurred speech or facial drooping (possible stroke)

## Home Management for Tension Headache
1. Take paracetamol 500mg to 1000mg or ibuprofen 400mg
2. Rest in a quiet, dark room
3. Apply cold or warm compress to head and neck
4. Stay hydrated — dehydration is a common trigger
5. Practice relaxation and reduce screen time

## When to See a Doctor
- Headaches occurring more than 15 days per month
- Headaches requiring pain medication more than 10 days per month
- Progressive worsening of headaches over weeks
- New headaches in patients over 50 years old
