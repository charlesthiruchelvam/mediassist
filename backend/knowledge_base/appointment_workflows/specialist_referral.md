# Specialist Referral Process

## When Do You Need a Referral?
A referral from a general practitioner (GP) is required for:
- Cardiology consultations
- Neurology consultations
- Oncology consultations
- Most surgical specialist appointments

Walk-in specialist appointments without referral are available for:
- Dermatology
- Ophthalmology
- ENT (Ear, Nose, Throat)
- Gynaecology

## How to Get a Referral
1. Book an appointment with a GP at our outpatient department
2. The GP will assess your condition and issue a referral letter if needed
3. Take the referral letter to the specialist registration counter
4. Specialist appointments are usually available within 3 to 7 working days

## Emergency Specialist Access
In an emergency, specialists are accessed through the Accident and
Emergency department without a referral. The A&E team will call
the appropriate specialist.

## Referral Validity
GP referral letters are valid for 30 days from the date of issue.
After 30 days, a new GP consultation is required.

## Out-of-Hospital Referrals
If you have a referral from an outside doctor or hospital,
bring the original referral letter and all relevant test results.
These are accepted at the specialist registration counter.
