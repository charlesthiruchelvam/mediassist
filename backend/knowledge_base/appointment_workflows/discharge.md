# Discharge Process — Patient Guide

## Before You Leave the Clinic

### Discharge Checklist
1. Receive your discharge summary from the doctor
2. Collect all prescriptions and understand each medication
3. Understand your follow-up appointment schedule
4. Receive any referral letters needed
5. Collect your test results if available
6. Complete billing and payment process
7. Return any borrowed equipment (crutches, blood pressure cuffs)

## Understanding Your Discharge Summary
Your discharge summary includes:
- Diagnosis and treatment received
- Medications prescribed with dosage instructions
- Activity restrictions and dietary advice
- Warning signs to watch for at home
- Follow-up appointment details
- Emergency contact instructions

## Medication Counselling
Before leaving, ask your nurse or pharmacist:
- What is each medication for?
- How and when do I take it?
- Are there any foods or activities to avoid?
- What side effects should I watch for?
- How long do I take each medication?

## Follow-Up Appointments
Most patients require a follow-up visit within 1 to 4 weeks.
Book your follow-up before leaving the clinic.
Do not miss follow-up appointments — they are essential for
monitoring your recovery and adjusting treatment.

## When to Return to the Clinic or Go to Emergency
Return immediately if you experience:
- Worsening of the condition you were treated for
- New or unexpected symptoms
- Side effects from new medications
- Fever above 38.5°C after a procedure
- Wound that is red, swollen, or has discharge

## After-Hours Support
For urgent queries after hours, call 1990 for emergency services
or visit the nearest Accident and Emergency department.
