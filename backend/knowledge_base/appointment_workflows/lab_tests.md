# Laboratory Tests — Patient Guide

## How to Request Lab Tests
Lab tests must be requested by a doctor with a lab request form.
Walk-in lab tests without a doctor's request are not accepted
except for pre-approved health screening packages.

## Preparation for Common Tests

### Fasting Blood Tests (glucose, cholesterol, lipid profile)
- Fast for 8 to 12 hours before the test
- Water is allowed during fasting
- Take regular medications with water unless told otherwise
- Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before the test

### Full Blood Count (FBC)
- No special preparation required
- Can be done at any time of day

### Urine Tests
- Collect midstream urine in the provided sterile container
- First morning urine is preferred for most urine tests
- Avoid excessive fluid intake before the test

### HbA1c (diabetes monitoring)
- No fasting required
- Reflects average blood sugar over the past 3 months

## Collecting Your Results
- Routine results are available within 24 to 48 hours
- Urgent results are available within 2 to 4 hours
- Results are released to your requesting doctor first
- You may collect a copy from the lab counter with your ID
- Results can also be accessed via the patient portal

## Understanding Your Results
Lab results should always be interpreted by your doctor in
the context of your symptoms and medical history.
Do not adjust your medications based on lab results alone
without consulting your doctor first.
