# Radiology and Imaging — Patient Guide

## Available Imaging Services
- X-ray (plain radiograph)
- Ultrasound scan
- ECG (electrocardiogram)
- Referral to external centre for CT scan and MRI

## X-Ray Preparation
- No special preparation required for most X-rays
- Inform the radiographer if you are pregnant or may be pregnant
- Remove jewellery and metal objects before the scan
- Results available within 24 hours for routine, 1 hour for urgent

## Ultrasound Preparation

### Abdominal Ultrasound
- Fast for 4 to 6 hours before the scan (water allowed)
- Avoid gas-producing foods the day before (beans, carbonated drinks)

### Pelvic Ultrasound
- Drink 4 to 6 glasses of water 1 hour before
- Do not urinate before the scan — full bladder required

### Other Ultrasound (thyroid, neck, soft tissue)
- No special preparation required

## ECG Preparation
- No special preparation required
- Avoid applying lotions or oils to the chest area
- You will need to lie still for approximately 5 minutes
- Results reviewed by doctor same day

## CT and MRI Referrals
We refer patients to accredited imaging centres for CT and MRI.
- Bring your referral letter and doctor's request form
- Inform the imaging centre of any allergies or kidney problems
- For MRI: inform them of any metal implants, pacemakers, or
  previous surgeries before the appointment

## Radiation Safety
X-rays use a small amount of radiation. The benefit of diagnosis
outweighs the minimal risk for most patients. Pregnant patients
will be assessed individually. Always inform staff if pregnant.
