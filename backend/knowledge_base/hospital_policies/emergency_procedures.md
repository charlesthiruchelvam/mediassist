# Emergency Procedures and Protocols

## Medical Emergency in the Clinic
If someone collapses or has a medical emergency inside the clinic:
1. Call out for help immediately — do not leave the person alone
2. Alert the nearest clinic staff member
3. Do not move the person unless they are in immediate danger
4. Our staff are trained in Basic Life Support and will respond

## Calling Emergency Services
Sri Lanka emergency number: 1990 (Suwaseriya Ambulance Service)
Police: 119
Fire and Rescue: 110

## Emergency Triage System
Patients are assessed by urgency, not arrival order:
- Red (immediate): life-threatening — seen within minutes
- Orange (urgent): potentially serious — seen within 30 minutes
- Yellow (semi-urgent): needs attention — seen within 1 hour
- Green (non-urgent): minor conditions — seen within 2 hours

## Code Blue Protocol
A Code Blue is announced when a patient has cardiac or
respiratory arrest inside the clinic.
- All available clinical staff respond immediately
- Reception clears the area and calls 1990
- Resuscitation equipment is available at all nursing stations

## Fire Emergency
- Activate the nearest fire alarm
- Call 110 for fire services
- Follow staff instructions for evacuation
- Do not use elevators during fire evacuation
- Assembly point: main car park area

## After Hours Emergency
The clinic operates during scheduled hours only.
For after-hours emergencies, please go directly to the nearest
Accident and Emergency department or call 1990.
