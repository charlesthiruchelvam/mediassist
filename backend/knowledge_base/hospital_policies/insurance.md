# Insurance and Billing Policies

## Accepted Insurance Providers
We accept most major insurance providers including:
- Ceylinco Life Insurance
- AIA Insurance
- Union Assurance
- Sri Lanka Insurance
- Softlogic Life Insurance
- Government employee medical schemes (GEMS)

## How to Use Your Insurance
1. Bring your insurance card and NIC to registration
2. Inform the registration desk that you are using insurance
3. Get pre-authorization for specialist consultations if required by your plan
4. Collect itemized receipts for all services for reimbursement claims

## Self-Pay Billing
Payment is due at the time of service for outpatient visits.
Inpatient deposits are required at admission.
We accept cash, credit cards, and bank transfers.

## Financial Assistance
Patients facing financial hardship may apply for the hospital's
patient assistance program. Speak to the billing department for details.

## Requesting Medical Records for Insurance
Medical records for insurance claims can be requested at the
medical records office. Allow 3 to 5 working days for processing.
A fee may apply per hospital policy HP-REC-001.

## Billing Disputes
For billing questions or disputes, contact the billing department
at billing@hospital.lk or call the billing hotline between
8am and 4pm on weekdays.
