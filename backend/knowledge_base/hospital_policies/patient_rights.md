# Patient Rights and Responsibilities

## Your Rights as a Patient

### Right to Information
- Be fully informed about your diagnosis, treatment options,
  and prognosis in language you understand
- Receive a copy of your medical records upon request
- Know the names and roles of all staff treating you
- Be informed of any costs before receiving non-emergency treatment

### Right to Consent
- Give or refuse consent for any treatment or procedure
- Receive information about risks and benefits before consenting
- Change your mind about treatment at any time
- Designate a family member to make decisions if you are unable to

### Right to Privacy and Confidentiality
- Your medical information is confidential and will not be shared
  without your consent except as required by law
- Receive treatment in a private setting when possible
- Have visitors of your choice, subject to clinical guidelines

### Right to Dignity and Respect
- Be treated with respect regardless of age, gender, ethnicity,
  religion, disability, or financial status
- Receive care free from abuse, neglect, or discrimination
- Have your cultural and religious beliefs respected

### Right to Complaint
- Make a complaint without fear of affecting your care
- Receive a written response to formal complaints within 14 days
- Escalate unresolved complaints to the Health Ministry

## Your Responsibilities as a Patient
- Provide accurate and complete medical history
- Follow the agreed treatment plan and inform staff if you cannot
- Treat all staff and other patients with respect
- Arrive on time for appointments or cancel with adequate notice
- Pay applicable fees in a timely manner
